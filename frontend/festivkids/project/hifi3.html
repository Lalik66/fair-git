Decision · Users
One screen, four roles.

Visitor, Vendor, Admin, Banned all share a single table with a pill column — easier than role-specific views, and admins switch contexts constantly.

Decision · Fairs
Cards over rows.

Fairs are big visual moments. Cards with cover art + a "+ create" tile communicate the calendar at a glance. Drill in for the deep editor.

Decision · Map CMS
Three-pane editor.

Tools left, canvas centre, inspector right. Snap-to-grid is on by default; layers map directly to public-side filters so admins see what visitors see.
