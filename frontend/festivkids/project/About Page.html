01Mission

Bring sellers
and visitors
into one lively
room.

Hospitality Local craft Small business Seasonal

FestivKids exists to bring sellers and visitors into one lively, hospitable room. Our mission is to celebrate local craft, support small businesses, and create unforgettable experiences for everyone who visits our seasonal fairs.

Each fair is a temporary village. Wooden houses arranged around a central square, vendors who know their craft by heart, kids' workshops on weekend mornings, and food that smells like the season we're in. Visitors can walk the map online before they arrive — so a Saturday afternoon never starts with "where is anything."

We work directly with the vendors. No middle layers, no opaque fees. If you make something good with your hands, we want a house with your name on it.

02 History

Five winters in.
And we're just getting
started.

FestivKids started in 2020 with one fair in Baku and twelve vendors. The simple idea: traditional craft, modern marketplace. Here's how we got here.

2020
First Winter Fair
Yasamal Park, Baku. 12 vendors, 4 wooden houses, 1 month. Two team members. Hand-drawn floor plan taped to a clipboard.
2022
Spring Fair · Ganja
First fair outside Baku. Added bilingual signage everywhere. Started recording each vendor on a real map so visitors could find them.
2023
The online map
Launched festivkids.az/map. Visitors could walk the fair before arriving. Vendor bookings opened a month earlier than ever before.
2024
Şəki Harvest Fair
Third city. First autumn-only fair. Local dairies and bakeries set the tone — and quietly became some of our most-loved vendors.
2026
Foxie · AI guide
Live now. Ask about workshops, vendors, opening hours — answers in EN today, AZ later this year. Still very much a beta.
